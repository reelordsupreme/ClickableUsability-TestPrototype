==================================================
THE PRODUCT
==================================================

Atlas is a modern investing education and simulated trading platform designed to make learning the market engaging, competitive, understandable, and interactive.

The core idea:

Most young people are interested in money and investing but traditional financial education is boring, confusing, passive, and disconnected from the actual market.

Atlas changes that.

Instead of only reading lessons, users can:

• explore markets
• research stocks
• paper trade
• build simulated portfolios
• follow market news
• understand why markets move
• journal trades
• use Atlas AI
• complete investing lessons
• compete in trading challenges
• compare performance on leaderboards

Atlas should feel like a product young people WANT to open rather than something they are forced to study.

IMPORTANT:

Atlas currently uses simulated/paper trading.

Do NOT imply users are trading real money.

Do NOT promise profits.

Do NOT use language such as:
"Get rich"
"Guaranteed returns"
"Beat the market"
"Make money fast"

The message is:

LEARN.
PRACTICE.
COMPETE.
UNDERSTAND THE MARKET.
